The dividend payout platform

At Apptware I built a dividend payout product from scratch as its founding engineer, owning frontend, backend and database end to end, using React, Next.js, TypeScript, Vue.js, Material UI, PostgreSQL and Drizzle ORM. This is what I built, in the order a payout moves through it.

  1. Bank details are approved

    Maker-checker (dual-approval) controls for changes to a shareholder’s bank account across ACH and wire, validating IBAN, ABA routing and SWIFT details.

  2. Payouts become a bank file

    The NACHA file generation flow that converts dividend payouts into bank-ready ACH batch files. It is the core of the product’s payment system.

  3. Shareholders see their payments

    A redesigned payments view: a mobile-first responsive card layout in place of an internal admin table. It leads with the net amount, shows the paying company and uses plain-language statuses.

  4. Tax forms get filed

    The architecture of an integration with a tax-filing REST API: an 8-table PostgreSQL schema plus a daily status-sync job, to automate IRS 1042-S filings and shareholder tax statements.

Around the build

Ran gap analysis with Product and Compliance stakeholders across tax reporting, escheatment and banking-detail workflows, and turned the findings into specs and interactive prototypes.

Found that disabling KYC broke tax-form collection and withholding, and fixed it with a feature flag that decouples tax forms from KYC.

Debugged production issues across the stack with Sentry and ArgoCD logs, including wire-payment schema drift between Drizzle ORM and PostgreSQL.
