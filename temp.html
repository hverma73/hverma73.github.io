The dividend payout platform

At Apptware I built a dividend payout product from scratch as its founding engineer, across frontend, backend and database, using Next.js, MUI, PostgreSQL and Drizzle ORM. This is what I built, in the order a payout moves through it.

  1. Bank details are approved

    Maker-checker approval for changes to a shareholder’s bank account, for both ACH and wire, with IBAN, ABA and SWIFT validation.

  2. Payouts become a bank file

    The NACHA file flow that turns dividend payouts into bank-ready ACH batch files. It is the core of the product’s payment system.

  3. Shareholders see their payments

    A redesigned payments view: a mobile-first card layout in place of an internal admin table. It leads with the net amount, shows the paying company and uses plain-language statuses.

  4. Tax forms get filed

    The design of a tax-filing integration, an 8-table PostgreSQL schema plus a daily status-sync job, to automate IRS 1042-S filings and shareholder tax statements.

Around the build

Ran gap analysis with Product and Compliance across tax reporting, escheatment and banking-detail workflows, and turned the findings into specs and interactive prototypes.

Found that disabling KYC broke tax-form collection and withholding, and fixed it with a feature flag that decouples tax forms from KYC.

Debugged production issues across the stack, including wire-payment schema drift between Drizzle ORM and PostgreSQL, using Sentry and ArgoCD logs.
